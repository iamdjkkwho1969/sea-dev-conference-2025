## City Tech Scene Summaries

**Singapore**
- Major global tech hub with strong fintech, biotech, and AI ecosystems.
- Key districts: *One‑North* (research & startup cluster), *Fusionopolis*, *HarbourFront* and *Marina Bay*.
- Home to many incubators (Block71, NUS Enterprise) and multinational R&D centres.

**Kuala Lumpur**
- Growing Southeast Asian digital hub; focus on fintech, e‑commerce, and gaming.
- Landmark areas: *KL Sentral* (tech office towers), *Bukit Bintang* and *Mid Valley* (co‑working spaces), proximity to *Cyberjaya* (Malaysia’s MSC).
- Active startup community with events like *Kuala Lumpur Hackathon*.

**Bangkok**
- Emerging tech scene centred on fintech, e‑commerce, and AI.
- Main tech districts: *Silom‑Sathon* business corridor, *Sukhumvit* (Startup Thailand hubs), *Bangkok Tech Hub* at *True Digital Park*.
- Growing number of accelerators and co‑working spaces.
